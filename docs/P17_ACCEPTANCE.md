# P17 Acceptance — C1→C2 Precision Bridge, Stylistic Control & Specialist Discourse

## Status

**Implementation complete.**

P17 is deliberately a **precision bridge**, not a claim that the product now certifies CEFR C2. It builds on the P15 authentic-source environment and P16 research-quality layer, then trains advanced control that broad C1 content coverage alone does not establish.

## Implemented

- Eight explicit precision-transformation modes:
  - compression without losing claim/evidence/limitation;
  - expansion of mechanism, assumptions and scope;
  - register shift;
  - stance/certainty calibration;
  - lexical precision;
  - cohesion and information-flow restructuring;
  - strong-counterargument integration;
  - specialist ↔ informed-public audience translation.
- Precision work stores the original text, revised text, rationale, target mode and optional P16 specialist-track linkage.
- Structural validation checks that the revision is substantial and that compression/expansion actually changes information density; it does not pretend to semantically grade the Japanese.
- P16 specialist tracks become the anchor for a five-function discourse cycle:
  - position;
  - mechanism + evidence;
  - expert challenge;
  - audience shift;
  - synthesis.
- Browser Japanese speech recognition can capture specialist discourse turns when available; typed responses remain explicitly labeled as a speaking proxy.
- One five-stage cycle is descriptive breadth evidence. **Sustained specialist discourse** requires every stage to be repeated after at least 20 hours, preventing same-session repetition from being relabeled as durable control.
- Fresh-source refresh tasks compare two exact P15 registered sources and require explicit analysis of:
  - what changed;
  - what still holds;
  - impact on the argument;
  - remaining uncertainty.
- Source refresh is learner comparison evidence, not independent fact verification.
- P16 human-review records feed a repair loop where the learner explicitly accepts, modifies or rejects feedback, produces a revised Japanese passage and explains the decision.
- Human-review repair remains non-mastery evidence.
- P17 state is derived from the existing StudyEvent stream; no second scheduler or mastery database is introduced.
- The C1/C2 portfolio advances to schema v4 and includes precision transformations, specialist discourse reliability, source-refresh work and review-repair evidence.

## Evidence boundary

P17 does **not** claim that:
- a structurally valid precision rewrite is semantically excellent Japanese;
- specialist discourse stage completion proves subject-matter expertise;
- browser speech recognition provides acoustic pronunciation scoring;
- source refresh independently verifies the factual truth of either source;
- a human-review repair automatically improves durable mastery;
- delayed specialist-discourse repetition is accredited C2 certification.

The P11 release-evidence track remains independent and unchanged.

## Acceptance checks

- Unit coverage verifies the eight precision modes, five-stage discourse architecture, structural compression/expansion checks, event-derived P17 progress and 20+ hour specialist reliability.
- Browser coverage verifies the P17 workspace, four advanced-work tabs and P17 portfolio surface.
- Existing P14–P16 regressions remain scoped to their own capabilities rather than the current phase label.
- The portable JSON/Markdown portfolio includes P17 evidence and explicit non-certification/non-mastery boundary flags.
