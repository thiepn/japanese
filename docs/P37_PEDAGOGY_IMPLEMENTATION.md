# P37 — Natural Production Consolidation & Contextual Review

**Stacked base:** `feature/p36-independent-production-conversation-continuity` at exact commit `77403c09caf02f04886a1ae4c26dc0e61313fafe`.
**Source-only branch:** `feature/p37-natural-production-contextual-review`.

## Substantial learner-facing implementation

1. **Sixteen qualified alternative Japanese sentence corrections**. Added original alternative phrasing and pragmatic explanation for each existing P36 correction item. Native `StudyPrompt` IDs, skill and FSRS cue families are unchanged; only authored `acceptedAnswers` expands. The role correction UI recognizes both existing and P37 forms, and reveals the alternatives and nuance *after* the learner has tried their own answer. Unknown but potentially correct free phrasings remain ungraded inside the role UI.
2. **Thirty-two additional natural response phrasings** across the existing P36 guided intent conversation paths (one alternate first and second reply for each of eight scenarios from both learner role perspectives). These are original A1–A2 Japanese examples, not new duplicate prompt IDs or a new graded engine. They are shown after the relevant attempt with notes on topic particles, polite phrasing, choice of verb, and response intent. All P36 guided paths remain explicitly scheduler-excluded.
3. **Post-example revision workspace** integrated into both the two-part guided conversations and inline correction. Learners may retain their initial phrasing, write a revised Japanese sentence, and compare text-level additions/removals of selected particles, polite verbs and expression patterns. The comparison explicitly avoids claiming grammar or semantic correctness. Source-owned revision events save only minimal owner-scoped `StudyEvent` metadata with `schedulerExcluded:true` and `rawTextStored:false`; neither the original text nor revised text is saved to learner storage. This is not another learner progress store or FSRS grading pipeline.
4. **Exact-scenario FSRS due correction review**. Added `dueP37CorrectionPrompts` and a new `buildP37CorrectionDueSession` connected through existing App -> J4Learn -> P27 Learn -> P33 RolePractice. When a learner chooses a conversation scenario, they see its truly due native correction cues and can open those specific prompts in the *existing* StudyPlayer. The action re-reads the active owner's MemoryTraces and returns the pre-existing unit lesson plus only currently due correction prompts, rejecting empty queues. Future-due or merely incorrect prompts are not misrepresented as currently due.
5. **Evidence-aware last-mistake status**. The existing exact-mistake cards now explain per prompt whether an FSRS card is currently due, scheduled for a later time, or has no valid due date, based strictly on canonical traces. Voluntary retry remains separate. Learners retain direct links to the right existing lesson stage and the exact originally missed StudyPlayer prompt.
6. **Listening-first production safeguard**. Previously hiding the Japanese partner transcript without playing the partner audio allowed a typed model match to generate independent recall evidence, even if the learner had not received a cue. The role player now accepts independent grading only when the partner's transcript is visible or the browser fires the speech-synthesis playback-start event for the *current* turn. This guard is in-memory; it does not claim listening mastery, synthetic speech quality or pronunciation scoring.
7. **Keyboard/mobile learning continuity**. As learners advance between the two Japanese free-response fields in a guided conversation, focus moves to the relevant next textarea. Existing IME-safe Ctrl/⌘+Enter and mobile-sized controls are preserved. The revision workspace includes accessible labels, separate graded/ungraded descriptions, keyboard submit support and layout adjustments.
8. **Concrete source defects addressed**. The inherited `buildP36RoleDueSession` unnecessarily filtered the already owner-scoped trace list using `MemoryTrace.userId` before matching FSRS cards; P37 uses the owner-scoped query's result directly after an active-account check. Post-example revision also rejects whitespace-only submissions through both keyboard and button paths. No persistent learner records, auth or database schema were altered.

## Rights, provenance and limitations

- All new Japanese examples and German teaching notes are independently authored. Marugoto PDFs remain general methodological references only. No original Marugoto text, diagrams, audio, lesson material or assets were copied or redistributed.
- All P23–P36 teaching material, existing StudyPlayer prompt identities and owner-scoped StudyEvents/FSRS MemoryTraces are preserved. The new features reuse Learn, Today and existing routing; no duplicate scheduler, mastery store or replacement screen.
- The initial/revised Japanese drafts in the new workspace are held only in component state. Persisted activity metadata is explicitly ungraded; no raw learner response/audio.
- Fixed-answer authoring cannot recognize every correct Japanese expression. Particle/verb substring differences are advisory textual observations, not a parser, universal grammar assessment or independent CEFR certificate.
- Browser speech synthesis might be unavailable or fail to start, in which case a hidden-transcript response is not treated as independently cued. Audio playback is not evidence of pronunciation quality.
- Due dates and latest-error review status are snapshots, not timed notifications or automated commitments.
