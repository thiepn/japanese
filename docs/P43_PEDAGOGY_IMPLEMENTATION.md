## Substantial original content

1. **25 original A1–A2 branching Japanese conversation scenarios** authored across `dialogue43-corpus-a.ts` through `-e.ts`. Every scenario has its own first-person situation, specific communicative objective, German instruction, Japanese partner question, two legitimate opening decisions, and one plausible wrong decision.
2. **Three genuinely authored, different continuations for each scene.** The direct decision triggers a direct partner reply and an outcome specific to that choice. The alternative triggers a different partner reply and a different valid outcome. The mistake triggers a new partner response requiring conversational repair. Follow-ups contain new Japanese writing goals, two contextually valid authored response forms, a concrete distractor, the reason it fails, and the consequence of resolving (or not resolving) that branch. No generic change of button colour masquerades as branching.
3. **25 separately authored future-context return questions**, changing object, situation or constraints without copying the guided scene's exact response. The first 100 source-owned cues are one independent opening-choice cue and three independent context-specific route writing cues per scenario; the 25 new-context cues yield **125 stable-ID canonical StudyPlayer cues** in total.
4. Coverage includes a cancelled train with bus versus taxi outcomes; reserving a missing book versus using its digital edition; keeping coffee while changing cake choice; shortening a museum visit versus returning the next day; rain relocation versus postponement; avoiding milk and excess spice; dining venue and bakery constraints; academic deadlines and room availability; appointment and hotel booking corrections; practical gift size and defective merchandise decisions; lost phone reporting; wrong bus direction; shipping speed; group-study coordination; language exchange duration; a late friend; photography restrictions; and choosing a useful volunteer task.

## Product implementation

- `apps/web/src/study/dialogue43.ts` exposes first-party immutable scene data and standard `StudyPrompt` catalog; `dialogue43-authoring.ts` contains a simple content-only authoring adapter (no player, account or persistence authority). All 25 scenarios retain a grounded P40–P42 base-case link by ID, without altering or duplicating earlier scenario IDs.
- `apps/web/src/design/P43BranchConversation.tsx` is a **guided React view only** mounted within the pre-existing Learn conversation stage. The learner chooses either viable opening or the misconception. The actual next partner utterance, correction required, and final resolution differ according to the selected route. An incorrect route response produces an explicitly unresolved result, with the option to restart and choose another response; completed branches show the specific authored outcome and per-choice consequence. Selected guided answers and history are ephemeral state and **never receive study credit**.
- `apps/web/src/design/P27LearningJourney.tsx` integrates P43 content in the existing **Verstehen, Antworten, Schreiben and Festigen** stages. A recent independently graded source-specific mistake can recommend the related P43 scenario. Source-specific branch-selection and writer actions reuse the user's existing Learn navigation.
- Writing repair opens the already-existing draft → revise → compare fields; it supports direct, alternate and misconception routes. Exact authored variants receive bounded context-specific feedback; all other valid possible Japanese formulations remain **unknown, not declared incorrect**. Optional source-linked `StudyEvent` records retain original user/device authority, `result:"skipped"`, `schedulerExcluded:true`, and no raw Japanese/audio; metadata contains only the authored scene/route and existing safe lengths.
- `study/runtime.ts` registers **all 125 new native prompts** for Today and Learn session extras. `study/journey27.ts` routes independent `branch:<id>` decisions and `branch-route:<id>:direct|alternate|repair` corrections to exact unit-authorized source cues using the existing StudyPlayer; no false notion of auto-branching free Japanese was introduced into the authoritative independent scorer.
- The 25 new transfer prompts flow through original `return:<id>` voluntary practice, `return-due:<id>` strictly FSRS-due practice, plus the inherited Today queue and per-unit FSRS review. The due status reads actual `MemoryTrace.card.due`, not a guessed interval or a previous incorrect event. Independent graded StudyPlayer responses create source- and owner-scoped evidence through the inherited scheduler and StudyEvents.
- `study/remediation36.ts` provides source-specific, **answer-free** misconceptions for mistaken opening decisions, wrong route continuations and later transfer; these open existing conversation/writing/review stages instead of new remediation engines.
- The original `p27-journey.css` adds responsive and accessible choices, consequences, route comparison, writing actions and minimum touch targets. No standalone router, telemetry, accounts, data synchronization, new database schema, AI grading or semantic simulator was built.
