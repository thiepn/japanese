# Unit 19 — Integrated Hiragana Reading

After Units 01–10 basic hiragana, Unit 11 early reading, Units 12–14 dakuten/handakuten, Unit 15 sokuon and Units 16–18 yōon, this isolated unit checks transfer to **real-word and sentence decoding** before a future katakana course.

- Stage A: 12 authentic mixed-reading words and four meaning/mora contrasts; checkpoint after 16 first responses.
- Stage B: eight short natural expressions and four checks of spoken particles は (wa), を (o), nasal ん + に, and mora timing.
- 28 first attempts and at most 28 independent correction records, without changing original first evidence.
- `wo` can be an alternate keyboard spelling for typed を in sentences, never an alternate spoken pronunciation in the multiple-choice phonological contrast.
- Isolated localStorage key, checkpoint reload, invalid-state fail-closed handling. Sequential stale-tab detection is not a true cross-tab transaction.
- No canonical progress, StudyEvents, Account/FSRS, media or production code modification. No pronunciation, fluency, retention, usability or Japanese-subject-matter certification claimed.
