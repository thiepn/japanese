# Japanese

A unified, local-first Japanese learning system in the THIEPN ecosystem.

## Product contract

Course, reviews, reading, listening, writing, speaking, authentic-input mining and future AI features must all emit evidence into the same learner state rather than maintaining independent progress systems.

Primary product surfaces: **Today · Learn · Immerse · Library · Progress**.

## Current phase

**P5 — B1 Expansion, Productive Language & Adaptive Immersion is implemented.**

The app now provides one connected Foundation → A1 → A2 → B1 → immersion system:

- reusable Study Player for lessons, reviews, listening, grammar, conjugation, sentences, connected writing, microphone speaking and assessments;
- complete hiragana/katakana Foundation with multi-skill evidence;
- **336 canonical lexemes** with meanings, readings, source provenance and native audio where verified;
- **86 grammar concepts** spanning beginner foundations through B1 discourse and inference;
- **209 linked sentences** and **40 capability-centered course units** in one course graph;
- delayed unit checks plus separate A1 and B1 five-area milestones;
- **22 canonical graded texts** spanning A1 through B1;
- **10 canonical productive tasks** with situations, target language features, model responses and reusable rubrics;
- connected writing through multi-line responses and transparent structural target checks;
- real browser-microphone speaking through Japanese speech recognition when available;
- listen → record → compare shadowing with local-only recording playback and self-rated pronunciation evidence;
- adaptive reading/listening recommendations derived from lexical readiness and current learner evidence;
- **56 additional exact-file-verified native vocabulary recordings** in P5, bringing the core to **203 canonical audio assets**;
- explicit audio license, attribution and native-speaker metadata;
- graph-aware graded reading plus improved B1 canonical/deinflected resolution for learner-owned authentic input;
- private paste / TXT / Markdown / SRT / VTT ingestion with lexical difficulty guidance;
- contextual vocabulary mining plus deduplicated full-sentence mining;
- private mined vocabulary and sentences returning to the normal Today queue, learner projections and FSRS scheduling;
- license-aware Tatoeba sentence/audio import retained from P4;
- persistent account-scoped learner data without copying learner-owned material into the public content package.

Current core content package: **v0.7.0 / schema 8** with 336 lexemes, 86 grammar concepts, 209 sentences, 40 Can-do descriptors, 40 structured units, 22 graded texts, 203 canonical audio assets and 10 productive tasks.

P5 deliberately distinguishes evidence types. Speech recognition is not an acoustic pronunciation score; shadowing ratings are learner self-review; structural writing checks are not full semantic correction; device speech synthesis is not native audio; and the B1 lemmatization layer is not presented as a complete Japanese NLP system.

The next development phase is **P6 — B1→B2 Independent Communication, AI Conversation & Advanced Feedback**.
