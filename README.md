# Japanese

A unified, local-first Japanese learning system in the THIEPN ecosystem.

## Product contract

Course, reviews, reading, listening, writing, speaking, authentic-input mining and AI coaching all emit evidence into the same learner state rather than maintaining independent progress systems.

Primary product surfaces: **Today · Learn · Immerse · Library · Progress**.

## Current phase

**P20 — Longitudinal C2 Readiness Consolidation, External Calibration & Final Advanced-Learner Qualification is implemented.**

P20 deliberately consolidates the advanced stack rather than adding another practice subsystem:

- a transparent eleven-gate internal qualification model spanning longitudinal duration, reliable long-form production, precision breadth, specialist durability, repeated interaction pressure, real human exchange, audio/listening breadth, repeated broad review, seven-dimension observation, stable external performance and reviewer-calibration resolution;
- a seven-dimension readiness matrix for lexical precision, grammatical control, discourse organization, interaction repair, register flexibility, prosodic control and listening under pressure;
- internal structural evidence remains separate from external human language scores instead of being converted into a hidden weighted proficiency number;
- every dimension exposes internal support, broad-review observation count, mean, latest score, trend, spread and persistent-weakness status;
- final readiness requires repeated broad human review across at least two reviewer labels and a minimum seven-day review span;
- reviewer calibration compares the latest useful broad-review pair dimension-by-dimension instead of averaging disagreement away;
- a three-point-or-greater reviewer disagreement on a jointly observed dimension blocks qualification until more evidence resolves the conflict;
- repeated low external scores surface persistent weak dimensions even when the overall average is high;
- the internal **Advanced pathway qualified** state appears only when all published P20 gates pass;
- dedicated JSON and Markdown readiness reports expose every gate, dimension, reviewer-calibration result and next-evidence action;
- the main portable C1→C2 portfolio advances to schema v7 and embeds the P20 readiness summary.

The evidence boundary remains strict: P20 qualification is an internal product state, not accredited CEFR C2 certification. Distinct reviewer labels do not verify independent reviewer identity or credentials. Internal structural evidence is not silently converted into semantic language scores, reviewer scores do not update FSRS/mastery automatically, and raw local P19 audio remains outside the synced/exported portfolio.

P11 remains an independent release-evidence track and can still report `HOLD_B2_RELEASE_CANDIDATE` until its separate external-review evidence requirement is actually satisfied.

See `docs/P20_ACCEPTANCE.md`, `docs/P19_ACCEPTANCE.md` and `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

The next useful phase should be **P21 — Final Product Consolidation, Real-Device Acceptance & Defect-Only Hardening**: freeze the learning architecture, test the complete Foundation→C2-oriented pathway on real desktop/mobile/PWA workflows, simplify accumulated advanced surfaces where necessary, close accessibility/usability defects, verify private-audio/account boundaries, and prepare a stable release candidate instead of adding another proficiency layer.
